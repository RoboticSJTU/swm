## Representation

- Use untyped PDDL: static unary categories in init and action guards, never :types or typed declarations. Declare :strips, adding :negative-preconditions and :equality only when used. Conditions/goals are conjunctions of atoms or atomic negations; effects only add/delete atoms. No quantifiers, disjunctions, conditional effects, derived predicates, numeric fluents, or durative actions.
- Give every action parameter a positive unary guard for its concrete entity category. Use the actual scene categories in action names and guards, not catch-all object, support, or container categories. If a necessary location has several concrete types, model the corresponding concrete alternatives.
- Use the simplest causally sufficient representation. Introduce structural or control relations only when needed to bind action participants correctly or express necessary dependencies. When a component's separate source or target role matters, bind it to its whole with part_of and put direct support/containment relations on the component.
- Model only entities and distinctions required by the current task; ground initial facts in evidence, not the needs of a preferred template. Express state and relation constraints directly, not as composite entity categories. Merge fixed doors, handles, and controls into their functional whole unless independent identity, state, location, or operation matters. Keep detachable parts and independently operated compartments distinct.
- Put access and lock state on the actual controlled unit, switch state on the device, pose on the moved object, and process results on the affected material/object. For a single-door appliance, open/closed belongs to the appliance. Track separate doors only when their independent opening matters; do not duplicate their access state on the whole.
- Use the predicate vocabulary below for matching meanings; extend it only for necessary new semantics, with fixed argument roles and a clear lifecycle. Omit redundant capabilities, scene details, and synonymous facts.
- Use auxiliary bookkeeping only when a necessary state cannot be maintained more simply using direct relations and proven reachable invariants. Bind the actual participants in every update; negating a relation for one arbitrarily chosen object does not establish global absence.

## Transitions

- One operator represents one completed manipulation or waiting event with its necessary participants, prerequisites, resource changes, and direct results. Keep independently completed acquisition, use, handoff, placement, and start/stop events separate; omit incidental contact and internal motion.
- Pick acquires holding and immediately removes direct source support/containment; place releases holding and establishes the target relation. Delete all superseded external location/alignment facts, including alternative modeled references, while preserving unchanged pose, carried contents and internal assembly relations.
- Distinguish only task-relevant material portions whose locations or processing results must be tracked separately. Transfer a discrete portion by deleting its source relation and adding the destination; preserve unchanged intrinsic results. Unlimited supplies use dispenses, but must not recreate the same tracked portion after delivery. Containing material does not imply filled; removing one content does not imply empty.
- clear means no modeled object is directly on that object. Preserve the moved object's clear during pick/place unless its own loads change. Adding on invalidates the support's clear; establish clear only after its last direct load is removed. Changes to in alone do not change clear. Track clear only where needed, and maintain it in every action that changes its direct loads. Do not require an entire multi-object surface to be clear merely to place another object there.
- Use uncovered only for a necessary opening-occlusion constraint beyond open/closed; ordinary contents need not obstruct it. Use opening_clear/closing_clear for the respective motion paths. Include lock and obstruction constraints only where physically relevant; express known blocking dependencies directly when this avoids redundant clearance state. Prevent placement into a carried object's own descendant.
- Use fill_<RECEIVER>_from_<SOURCE> when filling includes actively positioning a held receiver under the source. Require holding by the participating hand and preserve that occupancy. Keep transient alignment internal unless a later action needs it.
- For autonomous flow or processing, use wait_until_<OBJECT>_<RESULT>[_<RELATION>_<ENTITY>]* and require the necessary causal conditions. For flow, receiver placement/alignment must already be established; waiting does not acquire or position it. Include a hand only for a necessary sustained role and preserve its occupancy. Establish results at completion, never at startup or arbitrary shutdown; include automatic shutdown only when supported by device behavior. Keep independent start/stop actions separate.
- Retain necessary device-specific interlocks even when the task does not otherwise operate that device; do not add unused operating actions.
- Each hand has unit capacity. Account for acquisition, release, sustained control, and distinct simultaneous hands without unsupported hand preferences. Require accessible targets and acyclic support/containment; use (not (= ?x ?y)) only for roles that must be distinct.
- Keep only poses required by a later action or the goal. Omit transient tilt/reset inside pouring or other operations. A necessary pose change requires the evidenced old pose and replaces it with the new one; an unchanged pose is preserved. Treat a negated pose as another specific pose only when the modeled alternatives are exhaustive and mutually exclusive.
- Encode required operations and ordering through real causal dependencies. If final physical state cannot prove a required event, use a reusable event relation bound to its essential participants; no step counters, phases, or generic completion flags. Do not force independent actions into demonstration order. Check legal alternatives and illegal counterexamples; a solvable demonstration alone is insufficient.

## Naming and layout

OPERATION_OBJECT[_RESULT][_<RELATION>_<ENTITY>]*

- Use canonical operation words and functional categories, with relation roles ordered source, target, tool. For the same family, use the same fields and prepositions: pick_*_from_*, place_*_on/in_*, fill_*_from_*, pour_*_from_*_into_*, scoop_*_from_*_with_*, insert_*_into_*, remove_*_from_*, open_*, close_*, turn_on/off_*, wipe_*_with_*. Extend to new operations by the same rule.
- Include indispensable source/target support and containment context in names, including nested relations. Use in/on for positive relations and outside for required exclusion from a container; the name must match the condition's polarity. Remove noncausal context instead of naming it.
- Include fine placement regions or relative-position goals only when the instruction explicitly requires them; otherwise use macroscopic on/in states. Keep access, clearance, and supply relations that are necessary for physical execution even when the instruction does not spell them out.
- RESULT names only a necessary core result not already expressed by the operation. Include pose only when this action establishes that necessary result; a pose prerequisite alone does not enter its name.
- Use one name and causal model for the same operation and necessary context; compare parameter roles and state transitions, not variable names or ordering. Prefer an existing sufficient implementation, otherwise combine only causally justified conditions and effects. Do not merge by unioning all prerequisites or deleting necessary ones. Distinguish genuine differences in execution mode, indispensable object relations or required results with concise names. Local vocabulary differences alone do not imply different execution semantics.
- Use lowercase snake_case. Instances, appearance, numbering suffixes, and incidental context stay out of action names. Tools are separate handheld auxiliaries, not hands or built-in controls. Use *_top in names only to distinguish a container's exterior support from its interior.
- Order parameters by hand(s), core object, source, target, tool, then other necessary participants. List category guards first. Keep each precondition/effect on one line, with each old-state deletion adjacent to its new-state addition.
