## PDDL modeling

- Syntax: Use untyped :strips with :negative-preconditions or :equality when needed. Use conjunctions of atoms or atomic negations for conditions and goals, and atom additions/deletions for effects. For an action that must exclude a relation over every object in a proven scope, use :adl and a universal negative precondition such as (forall (?load) (imply (item ?load) (not (on ?load ?plate)))). Use disjunction only inside the scope guard. Keep goals and effects free of quantifiers. Omit types, existential quantifiers, conditional effects, derived predicates, numeric fluents, and durative actions.
- Parameters: Give every action parameter a positive concrete-category guard, not catch-all object/support/container.
- Abstraction: Ground init in evidence; keep distinctions needed for applicability, transitions, or task requirements. Removal/merging must preserve participant bindings, legal transitions, required outcomes, and processes.
- Components: Merge fixed doors/handles/controls into their whole unless independent identity/state/location/operation matters; keep detachable parts and independently operated compartments distinct. Use structural/control relations only for necessary bindings/dependencies; keep necessary source/target components explicit in those roles and bind them to their whole with part_of.
- Hands and tools: Pick immediately replaces source location with holding; place replaces holding with target location. Avoid unsupported hand preferences. Simultaneous hands must differ; use (not (= ?x ?y)) only for roles requiring distinct participants. Tools are handheld auxiliaries, not hands/built-in controls.
- Actions: Each operator completes one manipulation/wait with its prerequisites, resource changes, and direct results. Enforce required operations/repetitions/order through final physical states and causal dependencies.

## Naming

OPERATION_OBJECT[_RESULT][_<RELATION>_<ENTITY>]*

RESULT names only a necessary core result not already expressed by OPERATION.

- Vocabulary: Use lowercase snake_case, canonical operations, and functional categories for action/predicate names; exclude instances, numbering suffixes, incidental context, and irrelevant attributes. Use *_top only to distinguish exterior support from container interiors.
- Relations: Order roles source, target, tool; keep family fields/prepositions consistent: pick_*_from_*, place_*_on/in/at_*, pour_*_from_*_into_*, scoop_*_from_*_with_*, insert_*_into_*, remove_*_from_*, open_*, close_*, turn_on/off_*, wipe_*_with_*. Include indispensable support/containment context, including nesting; omit noncausal context. Use in/on/at for positive relations; name support/containment before relative position.
- Placement: Use pick/place for stacking/nesting and name direct source/target. Surface positions use place_*_at_<surface>_position.
- Poses: Include relevant participants and required poses in pose-dependent action names; name transitions by the resulting pose. Omit pose qualifiers that do not distinguish applicability or effects.
- Processes: Active filling uses fill_<RECEIVER>_from_<SOURCE>; autonomous completion uses wait_until_<OBJECT>_<RESULT>[_<RELATION>_<ENTITY>]*.
