Task
Extract the independent events supported by group G{group} of G{last_group}.
Instruction: {instruction}

{shared_rules}

Inputs
- Images: {frame_order}
- Earlier action names for continuity context:
{previous_actions}

Method
1. Inspect the whole group. Use the grid to compare relations and the individual images to resolve detail.
2. Describe each adjacent pair once in changes, in order: K0/K1, K1/K2, and so on. State the relevant visible start/end relations and uncertainty.
3. Assemble actions from the whole group's evidence, in physical order. An action may span several frames; distinct actions may share supporting frames.
4. Keep preparation and continuation in changes. Use actions: [] when the group establishes no new independent event.

Output
- Return JSON only, with exactly changes and actions.
- Each change has frames followed by change; frames contains its two adjacent K labels.
- Each action has frames followed by action (e.g., pick, open, turn_on, insert, turn, screw, push, press, pour, fold etc.). frames contains its three adjacent K labels.; cite at least two distinct supporting K labels in chronological order.


- Use a plain single-line action sentence, naming the object, source/target and hand as supported. Keep image references in frames; action text excludes |.

Example
Three frames jointly establish a pickup. Apply the same structure to the actual objects and all adjacent pairs in your group.
