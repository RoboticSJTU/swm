Given an instruction, an initial scene image, and a robot configuration, generate a compact, scene-grounded PDDL domain and problem for high-level robotic planning.

Input
- Instruction: {instruction}
- Initial scene image: attached
- Robot configuration: {robot_configuration}

Construct the task-relevant symbolic world model before writing PDDL.

1. Ground objects and the initial state
- Identify task-relevant objects and their quantities, including tools, supports, obstacles, and hands.
- Encode their categories, states, direct spatial relations, and hand occupancy in :init based on the image and the instruction's scene descriptions. Do not treat desired outcomes as initial facts or invent states to make the task solvable.

2. Encode task requirements
- Ground the instruction's required objects, operations, methods, ordering, and final conditions. Encode all required final conditions in :goal.

3. Build causal state transitions
- Construct a causal action chain from :init to :goal, including intermediate actions needed to satisfy later action preconditions.
- Express each transition as an action schema whose preconditions capture what must hold before execution and whose effects capture its direct, task-relevant state changes.
- Track these changes along the chain, ensuring that each action's preconditions hold in the current state and that the final state satisfies all goal conditions.

Output only in the following format, without explanations or Markdown fences:
<domain>
[complete PDDL domain]
</domain>
<problem>
[complete PDDL problem]
</problem>
